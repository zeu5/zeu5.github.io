Making Strassen-Winograd work on GPUs

2026-07-17

The following post is an ode to The Morning Paper. From 2016, the blog was a constant source of learning for me, making complex research papers from a wide range of top computer science conferences accessible. As I stepped into the research world, I came to appreciate more the immense effort put into producing detailed reviews and explanations week after week. Below I write my first paper review in the hope of being as consistent and also discovering new areas of research. I will be presenting "Compiling Strassen-like Matrix Multiplication Algorithms to Fast CUDA Kernels" published in PLDI 2026

General Matrix Multiplication (GEMM) underpins most of the deep learning (Image recognition, Neural Nets, LLM inference and training) workloads. So it is very important to extract the peak performance from hardware designed specifically for these workloads. So it is all the more surprising that the best performing implementation of GEMM is not the one that provides the best asymptotic complexity. The paper makes a step in bridging this gap - efficiently implementing an algorithm that provides better asymptotic complexity.
